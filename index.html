CoAuth - agent-native prior authorization

CoAuth lets a clinician and an AI agent complete a health-insurance prior authorization together on one live page, using the WebMCP standard. The agent fills and checks the payer form via typed tools; the clinician keeps clinical judgment and the legally required signature.

In Medicare Advantage in 2024, 80.7% of appealed prior-authorization denials were fully or partially overturned, and only 11.5% of denials were appealed at all (KFF) - most contested denials turn out to have been wrong, and most are never contested. CoAuth works on the filing and keeps the human in charge.

WebMCP tools exposed by this page

  • get_workflow_guidance - recommended sequence and safety rules
  • load_patient_context, check_payer_rules - read the record and payer policy
  • fill_field, attach_evidence - complete the form (typed, sourced)
  • validate_submission, assess_denial_risk, detect_conflicts - checks
  • draft_field, draft_appeal - agent proposes, clinician approves
  • submit - human-gated; blocked until the clinician signs
